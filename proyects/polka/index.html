Tiramiswag

(Tiramisu)

Ingredients

  • 4 yolks
  • 30ml of water
  • 80g of sugar
  • 300g mascarpone cheese
  • 200g heavy cream
  • 300g vanilla biscuits
  • 200ml coffee or coffee liquor
~ * ~

Instructions

  1. To make the sabayon: Place the egg yolks, sugar, and water in a bowl over a double boiler. Whisk constantly until it reaches 85°C or until a thick foam forms.
  2. Cool the preparation: Remove from heat and beat with an electric mixer until it reaches room temperature.
  3. Prepare the cream: Soften the mascarpone cheese slightly and whip the heavy cream to soft peaks. Gently combine both ingredients and then fold them into the chilled sabayon.
  4. In a dish or mold, place a layer of ladyfingers that have been lightly soaked in coffee syrup.
  5. Layering: Cover with a generous layer of mascarpone cream. Repeat with another layer of ladyfingers and finish with the remaining cream.
  6. Resting and presentation: Refrigerate for at least a few hours (ideally for a while or overnight) to allow the flavors to meld and the mixture to set. Before serving, dust the surface generously with unsweetened cocoa powder.

Chicakes

(Cupcakes)

Ingredients

  • 110g softened butter
  • 110g golden caster sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 110g self-raising flour

For buttercream

  • 150g softened butter
  • 300g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp milk

Instructions

Visit the recipe here! +
  1. Heat oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4 and fill a 12 cupcake tray with cases.
  2. Using an electric whisk beat 110g softened butter and 110g golden caster sugar together until pale and fluffy then whisk in 2 large eggs, one at a time, scraping down the sides of the bowl after each addition.
  3. Add ½ tsp vanilla extract, 110g self-raising flour and a pinch of salt, whisk until just combined then spoon the mixture into the cupcake cases.
  4. Bake for 15 mins until golden brown and a skewer inserted into the middle of each cake comes out clean. Leave to cool completely on a wire rack.
  5. To make the buttercream, whisk 150g softened butter until super soft then add 300g icing sugar, 1 tsp vanilla extract and a pinch of salt.
  6. Whisk together until smooth (start off slowly to avoid an icing sugar cloud) then beat in 3 tbsp milk.
