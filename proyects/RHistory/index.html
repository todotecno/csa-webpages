rugby information

1823: According to legend, at an English school in the town of Rugby, a student named William Webb Ellis had an idea picking up the ball with his hands and starting to run and this invented rugby.

  1. 1845: The first rugby rules are specified a measure that distinguishes the sport from football
  2. 1871:The Rugby Football Union (RFU) was established in England to begin governing the practice of the sport on British soil.
  3. 1873:There are no precise details regarding when rugby began to be played in Argentina, but there are indications that some members of the Buenos Aires Cricket Club were already practicing the sport that year in the Palermo woods, in the area where the Planetarium is now located.
  4. 1927:For the first time, the jersey is light blue and white. On the occasion of the visit of the English team (the second in history), Abelardo Gutiérrez (Gimnasia y Esgrima) proposed, and it was accepted, that our national team stop using white or blue jerseys and instead wear a jersey with horizontal stripes 10cm wide in light blue and white.
  5. 1953:The first South American Championship was held, in which Argentina became champion by defeating Chile (13-3), Uruguay (62-0) and Brazil (72-0). On November 29, 1951, the Union changed its name to the Argentine Rugby Union (UAR).
  1. 1964: Argentina wins the South American Championship in Brazil. Daniel Craven, president of the South African Rugby Union, travels to São Paulo to watch Argentina play in the tournament. Upon his return, he authorizes an invitation for the team to travel to South Africa the following year.
  2. 1965:The Pumas are born, and the milestone at Ellis Park. On June 19, 1965, in Johannesburg, the Argentine national team achieved a memorable 11-6 victory against the Junior Springboks, and during that tour, they were dubbed “Los Pumas” by the local press, the name by which they are known today. After the South African tour, the Section Paloise (the French champions) and Oxford-Cambridge teams visited Argentina. Rosario won the Argentine Championship, defeating Buenos Aires 8-6.
  3. 1972:The South African Gazelles are on another tour of our country, where Los Pumas lost the first match (14-6) and won the second (18-16) at Ferro. San Isidro Club, the local champion, drew with the Gazelles 13-13. The first Argentine Youth Championship and the first South American Youth Championship are being organized.
