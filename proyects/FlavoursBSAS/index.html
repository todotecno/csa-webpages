About the webside

This website brings together a selection of Buenos Aires restaurants that show different ways of eating in the city. For each place we share its official website and its most recommended dishes.

  • Four restaurants, four different cooking styles.
  • From classic steakhouses to chef-driven cuisine.
  • Three recommended dishes for each place.
  • Direct links to the official websites to make a reservation.

How to plan your food route

Follow these steps to enjoy the city through its flavors.

  1. Choose the cooking style you like most: steakhouse, chef's cuisine, local tavern or pizzeria.
  2. Visit the restaurant's official website and check the menu.
  3. Book ahead, especially for weekends.
  4. Order at least one of the recommended dishes at each place.
  5. Share your experience with us through the contact form.
