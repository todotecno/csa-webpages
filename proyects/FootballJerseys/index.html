The evolution of football jerseys

"How the different football jerseys were changing over time"

football t-shirt
  • The first jerseys were quite simple. They had long sleeves, heavy fabrics, and basic colors. They did not feature player numbers or names (1850).
  • Later, jerseys began to feature numbers, and distinct designs started to appear across teams to distinguish them from one another (1950–2000).
  • Sports brands began experimenting with synthetic materials, modern designs, and technologies to improve player comfort (2000–present).
