The Evolution of Football Shirts

Football jerseys have changed dramatically over time. From simple cotton shirts to modern designs, each generation represents an important moment in football history.

1950–2000

A New Identity

Numbers and distinctive team designs became increasingly common. Jerseys started representing the identity and history of each club.

2000–Present

Modern Technology

Synthetic materials and innovative technologies improved comfort and performance. Modern jerseys became lighter and more breathable.
