OUR SPECIAL CREATION

Vanilla Chocolate Chip Cookies

An original recipe of our own!

  1. Firstly, melt and brown 300g of butter. Then add 2 eggs and 300g of sugar into a bowl. Mix everything.
  2. Now add the dry ingredients: 400g of flour, 6g of baking soda, a pinch of salt and 150g of chocolate chips.
  3. After that, mix everything and make little balls of 47g each. When finished, place them on a baking tray.
  4. Then, bake in the oven for 15 minutes at 180 degrees. When they are golden brown, they are ready to eat!
